About Us

Nembo-Business Associate Ltd (NBA) is based in Bamenda and was founded in 2023/2024 by Nembo Joshua Ntsou to improve access to healthcare equipment for training, homes, and institutions.

We partner with customers and suppliers, valuing people over one-time transactions.

Our Vision

To become Cameroon's most trusted healthcare solutions company; improving access to quality healthcare products, empowering healthier communities, and building lasting partnerships that transform lives.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and affordable healthcare products and services while promoting health education, professional excellence, and innovative solutions that improve well-being.

Our Objectives

  • Supply of medical equipment and consumables
  • Procurement, distribution and supply services
  • Health promotion
  • Medical and public health research
  • Consultancy, installation, maintenance, support and training
  • Import/export

Core Values

Quality · Reliability · Accessibility · Service/Impact · Affordability

At NBA: Trust is our currency, quality is our promise, service is our lifestyle.
