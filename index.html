About Us

Nembo-Business Associate Ltd (NBA) is a healthcare solutions company committed to improving access to quality medical equipment, healthcare supplies, and professional support for students, healthcare professionals, homes, and institutions across Cameroon.

Founded by Nembo Joshua Ntsou, NBA began as a practical response to a growing need: many healthcare students were struggling to access the essential tools required for effective training and care. What started as a simple solution has grown into a trusted partner for reliable healthcare products and services.

At NBA, we believe that healthier communities begin with access to the right resources. Guided by our values of quality, reliability, accessibility, affordability, and service, we continue to deliver solutions that empower professionals, support families, and improve lives. Our slogan remains: For a Healthy and Smart Livelihood.

Our Vision

To become Cameroon's most trusted healthcare solutions company; improving access to quality healthcare products, empowering healthier communities, and building lasting partnerships that transform lives.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and affordable healthcare products and services while promoting health education, professional excellence, and innovative solutions that improve well-being.
